Section 6: Temporal split strategy (critical section)
WHAT: Define exactly how you split train_test.csv for development.
WHY: Required in report and Loom. Wrong split = overconfident model that fails on Nov–Dec.
HOW:

Primary internal split (recommended):

Train: Jan 2025 – Aug 2025
Validation: Sep 2025 – Oct 2025
Or use expanding window CV with 2–3 folds, always respecting time order.

What NOT to do as your main story:

Random 80/20 split across all months without acknowledging temporal leakage
Metrics to track on time-based validation:

MAE, RMSE, MAPE (careful with low rates), R²
Also MAE on rate_per_mile (often easier to interpret)
Optional: Compare random split vs time split side by side. If random looks much better, that confirms temporal drift and strengthens your argument for time-based validation.

Write a markdown cell titled "Validation Strategy" with a diagram or bullet list. This text can go almost directly into your PDF report.

Section 7: Feature engineering
WHAT: Build features used by all models.
WHY: Raw columns alone underuse geography, time, and market structure.
HOW: Build a build_features(df) function so train/val/december use identical logic.

Feature groups to consider:

A. Temporal

month, day_of_week, week_of_year
Cyclical encoding: sin/cos for month and day-of-week (helps tree and linear models)
is_weekend, is_month_end if patterns show up in EDA
B. Load / lane

rate_per_mile as a feature is leakage if computed from target. Do not use it as input.
log_weight, weight_missing_flag
log_distance
Equipment one-hot or ordinal (Dry Van=0, Flatbed=1, Reefer=2) after checking rate ordering
C. Geographic

Keep lat/lon for pickup and delivery
bearing (direction of haul)
mid_lat, mid_lon
Region buckets (Northeast, Southeast, etc.) from lat/lon if you want interpretability
Target encoding for pickup and delivery with time-aware CV only (encode using past folds, never future data). This is high impact but easy to leak; implement carefully or skip if unsure.
D. Market

market_index, quote_signal
market_index_missing_flag
Interactions: quote_signal * equipment, market_index * month
E. Lane identity

pickup + "->" + delivery as a categorical (high cardinality; tree models handle this well)
Or separate origin/destination encodings
Pipeline rule: All imputation, encoding, scaling inside a sklearn Pipeline / ColumnTransformer fit on training folds only.
